My Story

A long-standing curiosity,
now a career.

FROM HEALTHCARE
TO TECHNOLOGY

LONDON, UK

My route into software development hasn't been conventional — but my interest in technology started long before my career did.

I began my professional career in mental health nursing, a field that taught me how to communicate with people from all walks of life, solve complex problems, remain calm under pressure and make thoughtful decisions when they matter.

Technology, however, has always been something I've naturally gravitated towards. Growing up, I was curious about how the technology around me actually worked. I taught myself enough code to customise my Tumblr pages, experimented with modifying my PSP and spent hours installing and exploring mods for The Sims.

At the time, I didn't think of those things as the beginning of a career in software. I simply enjoyed figuring things out, changing how things worked and seeing what I could create.

As I progressed through my career, I began to realise that the direction I wanted for my future had changed. I found myself returning to something that had been present throughout my life — technology, creativity and the satisfaction of taking an idea and figuring out how to make it work.

That realisation led me to begin teaching myself software development. Today I'm developing my skills in Swift, SwiftUI, Xcode and web technologies while building my own projects including VIAME, ERA and LUNARA.

I'm still at the beginning of that journey, and every project gives me another opportunity to solve a problem I haven't encountered before, understand the technology more deeply and become a better developer.

My background has shaped the way I approach technology. I bring the curiosity that originally drew me to coding together with the empathy, resilience and human understanding I've developed throughout my professional career.
